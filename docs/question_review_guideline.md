# Question Review Guideline

## Source

- `original` and `internal` questions can be reviewed directly.
- `authorized` questions must include a source or license note.
- `user_recall` questions must be rewritten before publishing and must not claim to be official IELTS content.

## Part Style

- Part 1: short, familiar, conversational; avoid multi-clause essay prompts.
- Part 2: cue-card style prompt, usually starting with `Describe` or `Talk about`.
- Part 3: abstract discussion; invite reasons, change, society, comparison, or future impact.

## Quality Checks

- Check duplicate topic, Part, and wording before activation.
- Check difficulty is within 1-5 and consistent with the Part.
- Remove internal terms such as system prompt, developer message, hidden instruction, scoring rule.
- Confirm the quarterly release checklist before setting questions active.

## Non-Compliant Flow

- Block missing license or internal term leakage.
- Send style, duplicate, or difficulty issues back to reviewing.
- Archive outdated or unfixable content instead of deleting historical records.
